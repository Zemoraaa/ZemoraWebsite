The problem

Technology moves faster than our devices can be recycled.

Every upgrade cycle leaves a trail — old phones in drawers, dead laptops in closets, chargers nobody can match to a device anymore. E-waste is one of the fastest-growing waste streams in the world, and most of it never reaches a facility equipped to handle it safely.

Thrown in with regular trash, it leaks heavy metals into soil and water. Left in a drawer, it just sits on resources — copper, gold, rare earth elements — that could be recovered and put back to work.

What we do

We're the bridge, not the destination.

Zemora doesn't recycle e-waste ourselves — we make sure it actually gets to the people who can. We host collection drives, gather what comes in, and transport every device to certified recyclers.

01 / COLLECT

Host drives

We run collection points at campuses, parks, and community spaces where people can drop off devices they no longer use.

02 / SORT

Sort & log

Everything collected is sorted by type and logged, so nothing gets lost between drop-off and recycler.

03 / TRANSPORT

Move it out

We transport the collected e-waste directly to recyclers certified to process it safely and responsibly.

04 / RECOVER

Recycle & rebuild

Certified recyclers recover raw materials and route them back into manufacturing new products.

About Zemora

Built by students who got tired of watching devices go to waste.

We're a student-led organisation working to promote the recycling of e-waste in our economy — one collection drive at a time.

Our bio

Who we are

We are a student-led organisation that works to promote the recycling of e-waste in our economy. With the rise of technology comes a parallel rise in electronic waste — and we exist to close that gap.

We act as a collection group. We host collection drives and transport all the e-waste we gather to certified recyclers, so they can recycle it and turn it into new products. That saves our environment from damage and pollution, and helps build new devices without draining new resources.

Why we started

Two things were true at once

Devices were getting replaced faster than ever. And most people had no easy, trustworthy way to make sure their old ones ended up somewhere responsible — so they sat in drawers, or worse, in the regular bin.

We didn't need to become recyclers ourselves. We needed to be the bridge: the group that makes the responsible option the easy option.

Who runs it

Student-led, floor to leadership

Every part of Zemora — from planning drives to driving the van to the recycler — is run by students. No outsourced operations, no middle layer. If a drive is happening, students organised it.

That keeps us close to the campuses and communities we serve, and keeps overhead low so more of every drive goes toward moving e-waste, not running an office.

What we stand for

Our values

// Accountability

Every device we collect is logged and tracked until it's handed to a certified recycler. Nothing disappears in between.

// Access

Responsible recycling shouldn't require a car, a search, or spare time. We bring the drop-off to where people already are.

// No greenwashing

We only work with recyclers that are actually certified. If we can't verify where it's going, we don't send it.

// Student-run

Decisions are made by the students doing the work — not a distant board disconnected from the drives themselves.

How it works

From your drawer to a certified recycler — the whole route.

No guesswork, no dead ends. Here's exactly what happens to a device once it's in our hands.

01 / HOST

A drive gets scheduled

We set up a collection drive — a date, a location, and a drop-off window.

02 / DROP OFF

Devices come in

People bring in phones, laptops, cables, batteries, and other electronics. Everything is logged as it's received.

03 / SORT

We sort by type

Devices are grouped by category and checked for anything that needs special handling, like batteries.

04 / TRANSPORT

We move it out

Our team transports the collected e-waste directly to a certified recycling facility — never to a third party we can't vouch for.

At the recycler

What happens after we drop it off

Certified recyclers break devices down safely, separating hazardous components from reusable materials. Metals like copper and gold are recovered and re-enter manufacturing, while hazardous parts are processed under proper safety controls — instead of leaching into landfill soil and water.

What we take

Accepted vs. not accepted

We accept

  • Phones & tablets
  • Laptops & desktops
  • Chargers & cables
  • Keyboards, mice & peripherals
  • Batteries (taped terminals)
  • Small household electronics
  • Printers & routers

Not through Zemora

  • Large appliances (fridges, washers)
  • CRT monitors & TVs
  • Items with active liquid leaks
  • Medical devices
  • Anything without prior confirmation for bulk drop-off
FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to wipe my data before dropping off a device?

Yes — we strongly recommend backing up and factory-resetting any device with personal data before drop-off. We don't access or read data from devices we collect, but resetting is on you.

Is there a cost to drop off e-waste?

No. Drop-off at any Zemora collection drive is free.

How do you choose which recyclers to work with?

We only work with recyclers holding proper e-waste handling certification, and we confirm this before any partnership begins.

What if I just have one or two items, not enough for a drive?

You can still drop items off at our next scheduled collection point — check the Get Involved page for upcoming dates and locations.
